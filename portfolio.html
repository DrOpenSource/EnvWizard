To maintain speed, clean marketplace identity, and zero regression risk to GitWizard's existing userbase, here is the recommended repository strategy:

Recommended Strategy

Independent Repositories per Product

• Marketplace Separation: Open VSX & VS Code Marketplace require isolated extension IDs and distinct publisher configurations.
• Zero Bloat: GitWizard maintains a hyper-focused PRD (< 50 lines) and isolated CI tests. Mixing port management or packagers into GitWizard will bloat download size.
• Individual Sale / Acquisition: Each tool can be monetized, licensed, or open-sourced independently without licensing entanglements.

The Brand Synergy Play

Unified "DrOpenSource" Organization

• Keep separate repos (e.g. github.com/DrOpenSource/EnvWizard, GitWizard, DistroWizard).
• Cross-promote in the extension status bar: "From DrOpenSource: Unjam your ports with EnvWizard".
• Offer an all-in-one bundle pack (e.g. DrOpenSource Vibe Suite) down the line for single-license pricing.
